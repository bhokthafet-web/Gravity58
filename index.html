Customer Guide

How Browse Jobs Works

Post your requirement, receive quotations and select the right local business.

01

Create your requirement

Select Create Post, choose Customer Requirement, enter the work details, location, expected budget and your contact number.

02

Receive up to five bids

Local business owners can view the public requirement and submit their quotation, completion time and proposal.

03

Unlock your own post

Sign in with the account that published the requirement. Your owner access is recognised automatically and the Unlock Post link appears beside Share.

04

Compare the quotations

Check the business name, quoted price, proposed completion time and message before making your selection.

05

Accept and connect

Accept your preferred business. WhatsApp opens for direct communication and the requirement disappears from Browse Jobs.

Why does the requirement disappear?Once a quotation is accepted, the work is treated as assigned. Removing the post prevents unnecessary new bids.

Create a professional business card so nearby customers can discover and contact your business.

01

Create a business advertisement

Select Create Post, choose Business Advertisement and enter your business name, service category, description and location.

02

Add complete business details

Provide starting price, experience, contact person, phone, WhatsApp, email and one website or social-media link where available.

03

Your card becomes visible

After publishing, the business card appears in Browse Business based on its city, locality and service category.

04

Customers contact you directly

Interested customers can open your card and use the available contact number or website/profile link to contact your business.

05

You can also bid on jobs

Open Browse Jobs, select a suitable requirement and submit your quotation. A requirement accepts a maximum of five bids.

Keep your information accurate.Your business card is public. Use a working contact number and clearly describe the services you provide.
