How G58 Refills Works

Refills supports both repeat product orders and service bookings through one store link. Stores can send refill reminders, publish bookable services, manage experts and keep customers updated from request to confirmation. Here's how each side works, and your first month of store access is free.

Store Owner

  1. Create your store and share your unique store link with customers — by WhatsApp, QR code or in person.
  2. Customers who open the link get added to your store, and G58 reminds them automatically when their everyday essentials are due for a refill.
  3. When a customer taps to reorder, the request lands in your dashboard — approve it and fulfil like any other order.

Brand Owner

  1. Open a store's brand link and request to place a promotion card for your product on that store's customer page.
  2. The store owner reviews your request and approves the placement and pricing.
  3. Your offer appears to that store's customers until the promotion period ends.

Customer

  1. Open the link your store shared with you and sign in — no app install needed.
  2. Get reminded automatically when it's time to restock the essentials you buy regularly.
  3. Tap the reminder to send a repeat-order request straight to the store.
Customer Booking Guide

How to Book a Service on G58

Open the G58 link shared by your service provider. You can choose a service and available time, send your request and follow the confirmation from the same page.

  1. 01Step 1Choose Your Service

    Open the shared G58 link, browse the available services and select one.

  2. 09:30Step 2Select Date & Time

    Pick an available slot. Share your location only when booking doorstep service.

  3. EXStep 3Expert Is Assigned

    The service provider checks your request and assigns an available expert.

  4. ✓Step 4Get Confirmation

    See the expert name, confirmed date, time and live booking status in one place.

What you doChoose, schedule and send your request

Select the service and time you want. If payment is enabled, G58 shows the amount and payment step before confirmation.

What happens nextThe service provider confirms your booking

The provider checks the selected time, assigns an expert and confirms the request. Your page updates with the booking status and messages.
