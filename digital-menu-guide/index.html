Digital Menu

How G58 Digital Menu Works

Digital Menu turns any table into a live QR ordering point. Customers scan, browse and order — the restaurant manages everything from one live kitchen dashboard.

Restaurant Owner

  1. Build your digital menu — categories, items, photos and prices — from your dashboard.
  2. Print or place the table QR code G58 generates for your restaurant on each table.
  3. Manage live orders as they arrive, update the menu anytime, and track orders from your kitchen dashboard.

Customer

  1. Scan the QR code on the table — no app install needed.
  2. Browse the live menu, choose dishes and place your order.
  3. Track a live token as the kitchen prepares and serves your order.
