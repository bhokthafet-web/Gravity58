Privacy Policy

Read the GRAVITY58 privacy policy for Refills, Digital Menu, POS and digital business card data.

GRAVITY58 stores information you provide while using Refills, Digital Menu, POS or a digital business card — such as your store or restaurant details, orders, customer contact information and account details. Public business card information may be visible and shareable. Owners should avoid publishing sensitive information on public cards. Data removal and account controls are available through your dashboard or by contacting G58 support.
